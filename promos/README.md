# Plugin Store promo images

Marketing images for the Archie listing on the Craft Plugin Store, rendered in the same theme as
the plugin's marketing page at
[justinholt.com/plugins/craft-archie](https://justinholt.com/plugins/craft-archie).

## Building

```bash
./build.sh          # all slides
./build.sh "2 7"    # just slides 2 and 7
```

Output lands in `out/` as `archie-promo-N.png`, 1920×1080 (rendered at 2× in headless Chrome, then
downsampled so the type stays crisp).

## Screenshots

`shots/` holds the three control-panel captures, taken against `~/Sites/plugin-testing` with the
`archieBlog` and `archieTeam` blueprints applied. They are 2800px wide because they are rendered at
2× — the page is zoomed to 2 with the CP container pinned to 1400px, since the capture tooling here
has no device-scale-factor of its own.

The CP chrome is stripped before capture, not cropped afterwards: the global header, sidebar,
footer and the bars other plugins on that install inject are hidden, then `#main` is captured as an
element. Cropping instead leaves a sliver of someone else's notification bar at the top of a shot,
which is the sort of thing nobody notices until it is on the Plugin Store.

Nothing in the captures is edited. A panel that looks uninteresting in a shot is a sign the
blueprint behind it should be more interesting, not that the panel should be hidden.

## Editing

Everything lives in `slides.html` — one `<section class="slide">` per image. The palette and fonts
are the plugin site's: accent `#08818A`, Jersey 20 for display, Inter for body.

Three things bite when editing:

- **`.points li` is a flex container.** Every text run either side of an inline tag becomes its own
  flex item, so `<li>… run <code>plan</code> first</li>` lays out as three columns. Wrap the whole
  line in one `<span>`.
- **`background-clip: text` clips to the line box.** The gradient headlines need a `line-height`
  above 1 or their descenders are sliced off, which is invisible until you look at a `g`.
- **A tall screenshot gets `cpc-clip`, a short one must not.** The clip fades the bottom off at
  640px. Slide 8's caption points at the *Roll back* button, so that shot has to be shown whole —
  a clip there cuts away the thing the sentence is about.

`assets/` holds the fonts and two icon files:

- `icon.svg` — the app icon, used for the cover mark and the footer lockup. Copy it from
  `../src/icon.svg` whenever the icon changes.
- `watermark.svg` — the mark with the tile stripped, line work and all. Reducing it to the
  silhouette first gains nothing: at watermark opacity the thin strokes drop away on their own and
  what is left is the head anyway. It does need feathering, though — a filled head at full strength
  is a grey mass with a hard edge down one side, sitting over the copy rather than under it.

`fonts.css` is generated by `build.sh` (the woff2 files are base64-inlined so Chrome's `file://`
origin rules can't block them) and is not checked in.
